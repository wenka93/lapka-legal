Common Questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete All Data. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.

How do I manage my Lapka Plus subscription?

Go to your device Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel your subscription.

How do I share a pet with another person?

Open the pet's profile and tap "Invite co-owner". Share the generated code with the other person. They can join via the app.

How do I disable analytics?

Go to Profile → Privacy and toggle off analytics collection.
