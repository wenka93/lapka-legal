Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026 · Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lapka ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our app or visit our website at lapkawalks.com.

1. Who We Are

Lapka is a dog walk tracking app developed by Ivan Borisov (sole proprietor), United Kingdom. Ivan Borisov is the data controller responsible for your personal data under applicable data protection laws.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: johanintheuk@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data — email address, and name when provided by the sign-in provider or entered manually in your profile. Profile photo when signing in with Google. When signing in with Apple, your name is shared only on first sign-in and your email may be a private relay address.
  • Pet data — name, breed, sex, weight, date of birth, and photos.
  • Walk data — date, time, duration, distance, GPS route recorded in the background, photos, notes, and toilet activity log per pet.
  • Device and technical data — Firebase Installation ID, operating system version, and app version (collected automatically by Firebase).
  • App analytics data — usage data collected by Firebase Analytics (can be disabled in Privacy settings).
  • Website analytics data — when you visit lapkawalks.com and consent to analytics, Google Analytics may process page views, referral source, browser and device information, approximate geographic location, and clicks on App Store links.
  • Subscription data — purchase status and subscription details managed by RevenueCat.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app features: walk tracking, statistics, pet profiles, and shared pet ownership.
  • To sync data across devices and with pet co-owners.
  • To improve the app through analytics (if enabled).
  • To process and manage your subscription.
  • To understand how visitors use the website.
  • To improve the website.
  • To measure which website links lead visitors to the App Store.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — to provide core app features: walk tracking, pet profiles, statistics, shared pet ownership, and subscription management.
  • Consent — for app analytics data collection (can be withdrawn at any time in Privacy settings), website analytics data collection (can be withdrawn at any time through "Analytics settings" in the website footer), and location tracking (can be revoked in device settings).
  • Legitimate interest — for app security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

5. Location Data

The app requests Always Allow location permission to track your walk route in the background — including when the screen is locked. Location data is used only to record your walk route and is never shared with third parties in identifiable form.

6. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Active walk state (route, photos, notes) — to restore after unexpected app termination.
  • App settings: units, language, and notification preferences.
  • Walk reminder schedule.
  • Cached account data for faster startup.

7. Website Analytics and Cookies

The website at lapkawalks.com uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-HX10QCH0HW) to collect usage data. Google Analytics is loaded only after you explicitly consent by selecting "Allow analytics." If you select "No thanks," Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set.

After consent, Google Analytics may set cookies on your device to distinguish visitors and sessions. No advertising cookies are used on the website.

Google Analytics records page views and a custom event (app_store_click) that includes the call-to-action location and the App Store campaign URL. This helps us understand which parts of the website lead visitors to the App Store.

You can decline analytics or change your choice at any time through "Analytics settings" in the website footer.

Google Analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months. Google may process this data as a service provider under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Notifications

The app may send local push notifications for walk reminders you set up. No notification content is sent to external servers.

9. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage) on Google Cloud servers in the United States (us-central1). Firebase complies with Google Cloud security standards and industry-standard data protection practices.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is transferred to and stored in the United States. For users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, these transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) implemented by our service providers (Google, RevenueCat) in accordance with GDPR requirements.

11. Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers:

  • Google Firebase — data storage, authentication, and analytics.
  • Google Analytics — website usage and App Store referral analytics, enabled only after consent.
  • Google Sign-In — account authentication when you choose this option.
  • Apple Sign-In — account authentication when you choose this option.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management and purchase verification.
  • Open-Meteo — weather data based on your current coordinates. No personally identifiable information is sent.

12. Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data in the app.
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data via Privacy → Delete All Data.
  • Portability — request a copy of all your personal data by contacting us at johanintheuk@gmail.com.
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — disable app analytics at any time in the app's Privacy settings; change or withdraw website analytics consent through "Analytics settings" in the website footer.

To exercise your rights, contact us at johanintheuk@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (UK: ICO at ico.org.uk; EU: your national DPA).

13. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal data we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. To exercise your rights, contact us at johanintheuk@gmail.com.

14. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted from our systems.

15. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

16. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

17. Contact
